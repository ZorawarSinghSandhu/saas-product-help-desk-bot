​
👋 Welcome!
Welcome aboard! This guide is for team members who have been invited to use
Cal.com
through their organization.
​
✅ Step 1: Accept Your Invitation
Look for an invite link in your inbox to join your organization’s
Cal.com
workspace.
When you click the invite:
Create a new account or log in to your existing
Cal.com
account
Your profile will be automatically linked to your organization
You might also be invited to other teams within the organization, in which case, you can always visit
Teams
page and accept the invites.
🔐 If your organization uses SSO (Single Sign-On), simply log in with your work email.
​
📆 Step 2: Connect Your Calendar
If your organization uses
Delegation Credential
, your calendar may already be connected automatically. You can skip to selecting which calendars to check for conflicts.
Connect your work calendar to manage availability and prevent double bookings:
Go to
App Store → Calendars
Search for your provider and authorize access
Select which calendars
Cal.com
should check for conflicts (toggle them on) and where to place booked events (Set as destination Calendar)
​
📅 Step 3: Set Up Your Booking Page
Please check with your organization admin if they have already assigned events to you.
Your booking link allows clients and teammates to schedule time with you. Here’s how to configure it:
Go to
Event Types
Create or edit an event (e.g., “1:1 Coaching Session”). Note that any events assigned to you by your admin might be read-only and not editable by you.
Customize:
Duration (e.g., 30 minutes)
Location (Zoom, Google Meet, in-person, phone)
Availability (set working hours)
Buffer times and limits
🎨 Your company’s default settings and branding may already be in place — you only need to personalize if desired.
​
🔗 Step 4: Share Your Booking Link
After setting up your event types, share your link through:
Your email signature
Inside Slack or Microsoft Teams
On internal tools or client dashboards
Example:
https://cal.com/yourname/1-1-session
For round-robin teams or routed events, your admin may provide a team-wide link that automatically assigns bookings.
​
✉️ Step 5: Set Up Reminders
Cal.com
can automatically send:
Email confirmations
Reminders (Please check with your admin if they have already set up reminder workflows. In any case, you can create new workflows for your event types. You can create workflows by visiting the
workflows page
)
​
🔒 Step 6: Review Privacy & Security
Your calendar data is private — only availability is shared with bookers.
Cal.com
is compliant with major data regulations including:
GDPR
SOC 2
HIPAA (on request for healthcare orgs)
You can control:
Which calendars
Cal.com
can access
Who sees your availability
Whether your events are public or private
Read more about our security practices →
​
📊 Optional: Track Your Bookings
You can visit the
Bookings
page in the cal.com dashboard to view your:
Upcoming bookings
Past bookings
Cancelled bookings
And also mark guests as No-Show (
Learn more
)
You can also access the
Insights Dashboard
to view your booking related insights.
​
Leaving a team
If you no longer need to be part of a team, you can remove yourself without requiring an admin.
1
Open team settings
Go to the
Teams
page and select the team you want to leave.
2
Leave the team
Scroll down to the
Danger zone
section and select
Leave team
. Confirm when prompted.
Leaving a team removes your access to that team’s event types, routing forms, and shared resources. If your organization uses
seat-based billing
, your departure may affect the team’s seat count.
