Help Desk
Welcome to the official Cal.com Help Desk. To help get you started, we’ve put together a few resources to help you learn about how to use Cal.com.
This resource is for users of Cal.com and not for developers. For any self-hosted installation tutorials, developer docs and API references, please head over to
cal.com/docs
.
We strongly recommend to read the Help Desk when facing an issue or having a question before reaching out to support, but if you feel like your issue cannot be solved with the information provided, please visit
go.cal.com/support
or contact
support@cal.com
with your issue.
These guides will go into depth on how you can best use the hosted Cal.com for your needs.
